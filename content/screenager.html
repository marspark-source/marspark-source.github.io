Screenager

Overview
Remix of found footage, 2022. Made from early internet ads, home videos and people on YouTube talking about MySpace ads.
Problem
The web that promised to help kids learn also sold girls false images of what they should look like.
Question
How does growing up online inform the way girls view their own bodies?
Concept
How a tool built to connect people became a place where girls compare themselves.

Tracing the internet’s history to the MySpace era

The film starts with adults selling the internet for learning and family life, that morphs into a portrait of the modern woman that weaves themes of digital body image, capitalism, and identity.
