Overview
Sheet music is a set of instructions. It was never meant to be a picture of the sound.
Problem
Musician Imogen Heap’s “Hide and Seek” has no steady beat, and she stacks her own voice into chords. Standard notation flattens both.
Concept
Overlapping rings replace measures, so the song is drawn the way it sounds.
