Overview
This project explores how music apps push the playlists they want you to hear. Cubit finds the ones you want.
Team
Ella Grace Park, Daniela Gomez, Maryam Safai Fard

Building a tangible AI for playlist selection

Problem

Music algorithms are out of your control so you are left with whatever they play.

Question

What if you could have control of your music recommendation algorithm?

Concept

Curation for the mood and genre you already want by selecting a genre and a mood cube.

My role

I led the look and feel and the feedback loops, and developed the AI prompt that selects a playlist for every genre and mood pair.
