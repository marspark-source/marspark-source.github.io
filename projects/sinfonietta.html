Overview
This project looks for a more intentional, ritual activity that re-engages surroundings and the senses.

Building an instrument for sampling the environment

Problem

Our devices are always in our hands distracting us from observing our surroundings.

Question

What if paying attention to ambient sound was as easy as paying attention to our distractions?

Concept

Sinfonietta is an instrument that turns the environmental soundscape into musical remixes that you can loop and create your own compositions from using physical sound filters.

Role

This was an independent project where I built the working prototype connecting an NFC reader to an Arduino Uno. Additionally, I developed the concept with sound experts, from classically trained musicians to sound design specialists and created the videos to explain the project.

Where to take the project next

From the chosen form, it could move to softer materials and more organic shapes.

Hand radio

A soft, modular radio for your grip. Make it longer or shorter.

Pendant

A wearable pendant with sensing tech. Put in a circular card, turn it from min to max.

Twistable radio

A soft radio you twist for different signals, from closed to open.

To understand forms beyond screens, prototyping objects moved away from the working prototype that was connected to a computer. Options that stand out for more exploration require more time understanding softer materials.
