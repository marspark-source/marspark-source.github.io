Screenager

What
A film remixed from found footage
Date
2022
Context
Solo, NYU Tisch Film
Overview
A film made from early internet ads, home videos and YouTube users talking about MySpace.
Problem
The web that promised to help kids learn also sold girls false images of what they should look like.
Question
How does growing up online inform the way girls view their own bodies?
Concept
How a tool built to connect people became a place where girls compare themselves.

Tracing the internet’s history to the MySpace era

Internet on the news

“It’s getting bigger and bigger all the time.”

A news anchor

Internet for shopping

“Instead of standing in line, I just sit here and punch one button.”

A man

Internet in the home

“Having the internet in our home has had a great impact on our lives.”

A family

Internet for reaching out

“Is anybody else willing to help me figure out how I can take these ads off?”

A teenage girl

The film starts with adults selling the internet for learning and family life. It becomes a portrait of the modern woman, weaving together digital body image, capitalism and identity.
