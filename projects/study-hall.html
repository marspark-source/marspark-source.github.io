Study Hall

What
Chrome extension for finding YouTube study rooms, with a focus island
Date
2026
Context
Solo, personal project
Overview
People use background playlist videos as a virtual study hall, a room to step into and focus. Study Hall finds the room that matches your mood and saves the distractions for your break.

Finding a space to focus in the noise

Problem

What helps you focus is personal. On YouTube you have to search for it, and that search becomes the distraction.

Question

How can YouTube, a place full of distractions and rabbit holes, be curated for focus?

Concept

A Chrome extension for YouTube that helps you find your focus tuned to your mood and energy.

Each time a new mood selection is picked (1), a new room is generated (2). Save (3) a room that you want to return to in future focus sessions (4).

Videos outside your study playlist are grayed out. Hover to preview one, clicktap, and you can save it as a reward with a burst of sparkles.

Saved videos wait on YouTube for your break. The island stays on screen as a reminder that studying is your choice. ClickTap it to return.
